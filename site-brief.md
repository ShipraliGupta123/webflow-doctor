# Brewed — demo site brief

Brewed is a one-page store for coffee gear at https://webflow-doctor-demo.webflow.io.

## Requirements
- The home page hero shows the heading "Brewed — small-batch coffee gear".
- The hero image has descriptive alt text for accessibility.
- A "Shop now" call-to-action button is visible in the hero on desktop AND on
  mobile viewports (390px wide and below), and clicking it leads to the products section.
- The products section lists 3 products (Pour-over Kettle, Burr Grinder, Travel Press),
  each with an image, name, and description.
- Every product's "Buy" button leads to a working page (no 404 or error page).
- The footer shows a "Get in touch" heading and a contact email link.
